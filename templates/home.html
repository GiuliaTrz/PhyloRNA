PhyloRNA: a database of RNA secondary structures with associated phylogeny

PhyloRNA contains known RNA secondary structures associated with their phylogenetic information according to several curated taxonomies (SILVA, ENA, LTP, NCBI, GTDB) to allow the scientific community to study the relationship between RNA structures and phylogenetic ranks.

In addition, structural features and abstractions of the structures are available.
