Disclaimer

The use of PhyloRNA is free of charge. The authors have taken all necessary steps to ensure that the database and website are implemented correctly and perform their intended function as described in the documentation. However, neither the authors nor the University of Camerino guarantee accuracy, suitability for a particular purpose, or the continued availability of PhyloRNA.

Condition of use

PhyloRNA currently consists of RNA secondary structures from various sources. For each RNA sequence, we provide the corresponding BPSEQ file, FASTA file, dot-bracket (DB) file, and CT file. All files are available for download after a search. Additionally, a CSV file is provided, containing complete information, including phylogeny, about each downloaded RNA secondary structure.

Updates

The first version of PhyloRNA went online in December 2025.

The phylogeny information provided by SILVA, ENA, LTP, NCBI, and GTDB was updated as of December 2025.

Future updates are scheduled every six months and will be reported on this page.
